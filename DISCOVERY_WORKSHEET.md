# Pre-sales discovery worksheet

**Customer:** fictional example only  
**Discovery objective:** Determine whether a document-management migration is technically feasible and economically justified.

## Questions to ask
1. Which DMS platform and version are in use? What exports and APIs exist?
2. How much data is stored? What is the annual growth rate?
3. Who needs access, and which permission groups must be preserved?
4. What legal holds, retention policies, encryption, residency, and audits apply?
5. What is the maximum acceptable downtime and recovery time?
6. How are licenses, infrastructure, backups, and admin costs billed today?
7. What integrations must keep working (email, matter management, SSO)?
8. Who signs off on a pilot and what does success look like?

## Success criteria for a proof of concept
- A synthetic sample dataset can be transferred and validated.
- Permissions mapping is demonstrated for a representative set of roles.
- A written cost model states assumptions and exclusions.
- A customer stakeholder can explain the tradeoffs and next steps.

## Next-step proposal
1. Run technical discovery with DMS administrator and IT lead.
2. Validate storage tier, retention and migration vendor licensing.
3. Compare provider-specific price quotes with this illustrative estimate.
4. Pilot with synthetic records before handling any actual customer data.
